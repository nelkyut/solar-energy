⚠️ Important safety note
These calculations are preliminary estimates, not an installation design or safety certification. Verify the exact SCC maximum PV open-circuit voltage, MPPT operating range, permitted PV wattage, battery charging limits, inverter surge rating, BMS limits, cable sizes, grounding, disconnects, and fuse/breaker ratings against the actual manufacturers' specifications. Cold-weather Voc must use the panel's temperature coefficient and the expected minimum cell temperature. Have the system checked by a qualified solar installer or electrician before energizing.
